I went out intending to shoot one of the rolls of B&W that I had collecting dust. I brought with me my Miranda Camera, a 28mm lens, a 105mm lens, and a focal length doubler so I essentially had a 52mm lens and a 210mm lens with me as well. I made good use of the various combinations, switching frequently to find the best framing for the shot I wanted to capture.<br>
I stopped by Central Camera to drop off the previous two rolls to get developed, and I picked up another roll of Fuji 400 expecting to shoot it on another day. By the time I finished my roll of Ilford, there was still enough daylight, and I was having enough fun, that I decided to pop in the roll of Fuji I had just bought and keep the day going. I then shot the whole roll of Fuji 400 by the time it got dark and I headed home.<br>
I really had a lot of fun walking around downtown and looking for interesting photos to try to capture. I should try to remember this feeling when photography feels like a chore.<br>
<br>
Shot on Ilford XP2 Super 400 & Fuji 400.
