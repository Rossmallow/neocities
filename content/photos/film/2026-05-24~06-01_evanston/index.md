I went out to Evanston with my friend Rui to shoot some photos since it had been quite a while since the last time I had. It was fun to be taking photos again.
One thing that I was worried about during that shoot was whether my camera's light meter was functioning properly. I was adjusting my settings to match the internal light meter, but the values didn't seem to make sense to me based on how much light was out. I was afraid that all of my images were going to be useless. I did a few comparison shots with my phone's light meter after that, and comparing them to my camera's meter, my camrea might be slightly overexposing the image, but in general, I think that might be okay and I do think I tend to prefer the look of an overexposed image. I may continue to do some testing, but I'm not going to worry about avoiding my camera's meter anymore.<br>
<br>
Shot on Fuji 400.
